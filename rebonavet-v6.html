Biomaterial Science

How β-TCP
becomes bone

Sequential biological events guide the scaffold from temporary matrix to permanent native bone — with no residual foreign material at the healed site.

I

Osteoconductive Scaffold

Interconnected macroporosity and calcium phosphate chemistry guide cell attachment, proliferation and vascular ingrowth — structurally analogous to cancellous bone mineral.

II

Silicate Bioactivation

Silicon ions upregulate osteogenic pathways and increase protein retention — converting the scaffold from osteoconductive to osteoinductive without additional growth factors.

III

Complete Resorption

Progressive β-TCP degradation over 4–6 months is matched to new bone apposition — ensuring structural continuity with zero residual synthetic material.
